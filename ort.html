Core Training Program

ORT

Operational Readiness Training — pressure-based training that forces execution under real conditions. No comfort zones.

What it is

ORT is the advanced core program of Infinity Studio. After Foundations builds communication capacity, ORT applies it under operational pressure — simulating the demands of BPO, customer service, tech support and international remote roles.

How it works

  • Realistic scenarios with time pressure and performance expectations
  • KPI-driven feedback from your trainer
  • Personalized coaching and structured follow-up
  • Exercises targeted to your operational weaknesses

Not a community program

ORT is structured training — distinct from satellite initiatives like Off The Clock (talk show), The Conversatory (live practice events) and Job Finder (employability).
