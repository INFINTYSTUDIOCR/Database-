1. Rol del asistente

Apoyás al capitán: guests, checklists, radio y comunicación clara. Nunca inventés datos de navegación — confirmá con el bridge.

  • Passenger count y headcount before departure
  • Safety gear visible and explained
  • Bridge ↔ guests: vos sos el puente de lenguaje
  • Emergencias: instrucciones cortas, una a la vez
Say this · Welcome aboard
Welcome aboard the Pacific Star. I’m Karina, assistant to Captain Morales. Please watch your step on the gangway and hold the handrail.
Bienvenidos a bordo del Pacific Star. Soy Karina, asistente del Capitán Morales…

2. Boarding & headcount

Escenario
Tour de 18 guests. Falta 1 persona del manifiesto. El capitán espera green light.
Say this · Manifest
May I have your last name for the passenger list, please?
We’re missing one guest from the manifest. Please stay near the dock — we’ll depart as soon as everyone is accounted for.
Captain, headcount is seventeen of eighteen. Still waiting on Mr. Hale.
¿Me da su apellido para la lista de pasajeros?…

3. Safety briefing (antes de zarpar)

Escenario · Mandatory
Grupo mixto EN/ES. Algunos no ponen atención.
Say this · Safety brief
Before we leave the dock, a short safety briefing.
Life jackets are under your seats — this is how you put one on. In an emergency, remain calm and follow crew instructions.
No running on deck. Keep hands inside the railings. If you feel seasick, please tell a crew member immediately.
Antes de salir del muelle, un breve briefing de seguridad…
Hablá despacio. Mostrá el chaleco mientras hablás. Preguntá: “Any questions before we cast off?”

4. Radio & bridge language

Frases cortas, números claros, sin chistes en canal operativo.

Say this · Radio style
Bridge, this is deck. Passenger count confirmed: eighteen. Ready for departure.
Captain, guest reports mild seasickness on the starboard side — requesting ginger candy and a seat midship.
Affirmative. I’ll inform the guests we’ll delay five minutes due to traffic in the channel.
  • Affirmative / Negative — más claro que yes/no en ruido
  • Port / Starboard — left / right facing forward
  • Stand by — esperá instrucción

5. Clima, itinerario y cambios

Escenario
Swell sube. Capitán cancela snorkel; ofrece coastal cruise + snack.
Say this · Change of plan
Due to higher waves, the captain has canceled snorkeling for safety.
We’ll continue with a coastal cruise and a snack service. Your tickets remain valid for a make-up snorkel on another day — I’ll explain how to rebook at the dock.
Por el oleaje, el capitán canceló el snorkel por seguridad…

6. Emergencias a bordo

Escenario · Critical
Guest se resbala; dolor en tobillo. Otro grita “man overboard” (falsa alarma — solo sombrero).
Say this · Emergency calm
Please stay seated. Medical assistance is coming to you now.
Bridge — we have an injured guest on the aft deck, possible ankle injury. Requesting first-aid kit and slow speed.
Attention guests: that was a hat in the water, not a person. Thank you for your alertness. Please remain clear of the railings.
En emergencia real: 1) seguridad 2) avisar bridge 3) una instrucción a la vez a los guests.

7. Docking & farewell

Say this · Return to dock
We’re approaching the dock. Please remain seated until the crew secures the lines.
Watch your step on the gangway. Don’t forget your belongings under the seats.
On behalf of Captain Morales and the crew, thank you for sailing with us today. Have a safe trip home.
