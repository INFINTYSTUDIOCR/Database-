Recepción de hotel

Front desk en inglés profesional: check-in/out, upgrades, quejas, late arrivals y billing. Escenarios diarios + frases listas.

LecciónCertificación 80%

1. Rol y registro profesional

Sos la cara del hotel. Sonás calmada, clara y útil. Nunca improvisés el nombre del guest — confirmalo.

  • Saludo + nombre del hotel + oferta de ayuda
  • Confirmá spelling del apellido y número de reserva
  • Explicá amenities en 2–3 frases máximo
  • Cierre: “Is there anything else I can help you with?”
Say this · Opening
Good afternoon, and welcome to Pacific Bay Hotel. My name is Karina. How may I assist you today?
Buenas tardes, bienvenido a Pacific Bay Hotel. Mi nombre es Karina. ¿En qué le puedo ayudar?

2. Check-in diario

Escenario
Guest: Mr. Daniel Brooks · Reservation #48219 · 2 nights · King room
Llega a las 3:10 p.m. Quiere late checkout mañana y parking.
  1. Ask for ID and the card used for the reservation
  2. Confirm dates, room type, rate, and breakfast if included
  3. Issue key cards and explain breakfast / Wi-Fi / checkout time
  4. Offer late checkout (if available) and parking instructions
Say this · Check-in flow
May I have a photo ID and the credit card used for the reservation, please?
I have you for two nights in a king room. Breakfast is included from 6:30 to 10:00 a.m. Checkout is at 11:00 a.m.
I can note a late checkout request for noon, subject to availability. Parking is on level B1 — please keep the ticket with you.
¿Me puede mostrar una identificación y la tarjeta con la que reservó? Tiene dos noches en habitación king. El desayuno está incluido…
Si el guest habla rápido: “Just to confirm…” y repetí fechas + room type.

3. Late arrival & no reservation

Escenario
Guest: Ms. Elena Vargas · vuelo retrasado · 11:40 p.m.
No encuentra la confirmación en el correo. Está cansada y un poco molesta.
Say this · Empathy + solve
I’m sorry for the delay with your flight. Let me look that up under your last name and the dates of stay.
I found a guaranteed reservation under Vargas for tonight. I’ll check you in right away and have housekeepers refresh the room if needed.
Lamento el retraso del vuelo. Déjeme buscarlo por apellido y fechas…

Si no hay reserva: ofrecé walk-in rate, room types disponibles, o hoteles hermanos cercanos — nunca digas solo “no hay”.

4. Upgrade, room change & amenities

Escenario
Guest pide vista al mar; solo hay ocean-view por $45/night upgrade. También quiere extra pillows y iron.
Say this · Upsell sin presión
I can move you to an ocean-view room for an additional forty-five dollars per night. Would you like me to apply that upgrade?
I’ll also send two extra pillows and an iron to your room within twenty minutes.
Puedo cambiarle a vista al mar por cuarenta y cinco dólares adicionales por noche…

5. Quejas y service recovery

Escenario · Difficult
Guest: A/C noise, shower pressure low, and “the room smells like paint.” Quiere discount.
  1. Acknowledge + apologize (no excuses first)
  2. Action: inspect / move room / engineering
  3. Compensation within policy (amenity, F&B credit, rate adjustment)
  4. Follow-up call or note on the folio
Say this · Recovery
I’m truly sorry about the noise and the water pressure. That is not the experience we want for you.
I can move you to a quiet room on a higher floor right now, and I’ll add a twenty-dollar credit to your account for dinner.
Lamento mucho el ruido y la presión del agua…

6. Check-out & billing

Escenario
Guest disputa un cargo de minibar $18 y un late checkout $30.
Say this · Billing calm
Of course — let’s review the folio together line by line.
The minibar charge is from room service at 9:14 p.m. If that was a mistake, I can remove it after verifying with housekeeping.
The late checkout fee applies after 12:00 p.m. Since you returned the key at 11:50, I can waive that charge.
Claro — revisemos el folio línea por línea…
Nunca pelees el cargo en público. Bajá la voz, mostrá el folio, ofrecé solución dentro de política.

7. Lost luggage & special requests

Escenario
Airline perdió la maleta. Guest necesita amenities kit, laundry info y taxi al aeropuerto mañana a las 5:00 a.m.
Say this · Care package
I’m sorry about your luggage. I’ll prepare a complimentary amenities kit and a toothbrush set for your room.
Laundry closes at 8:00 p.m.; express service is available for an extra fee.
I can arrange a taxi for 5:00 a.m. — shall I confirm it under your name?
