1. Approach & first impression

Sonreí, presentate, ofrecé agua y el menú. Preguntá idioma sin avergonzar: “Would you prefer English or Spanish?”

Say this · Approach
Good evening. Welcome to Casa Marina. My name is Karina and I’ll be taking care of you tonight. May I start you with still or sparkling water?
Buenas noches. Bienvenidos a Casa Marina. Me llamo Karina y los voy a atender esta noche. ¿Les ofrezco agua sin gas o con gas?

2. Tomar la orden (appetizers → mains → drinks)

Escenario
Table of 3 · tourist couple + local friend
Uno pide “the fish special,” otro steak medium-rare, la tercera es vegetariana.
  1. Repeat back every item + cooking preference
  2. Ask about sides and sauces
  3. Confirm allergies before sending to the kitchen
  4. Thank them and give a time estimate
Say this · Order confirm
Just to confirm: one catch of the day with rice, one ribeye medium-rare with mashed potatoes, and one vegetarian pasta without cheese. Is that correct?
The mains will be about eighteen to twenty minutes. Enjoy!
Solo para confirmar: un pescado del día con arroz, un ribeye medium-rare…

3. Alergias y dietas

Escenario · Safety
Guest: “I’m allergic to shellfish and peanuts.” Quiere ceviche — el menú usa camarón.
Say this · Allergy protocol
Thank you for telling me. I’ll flag shellfish and peanut allergies with the kitchen right away.
Our ceviche contains shrimp, so I don’t recommend it. May I suggest the grilled catch with vegetables instead? I’ll confirm there’s no cross-contamination.
Gracias por avisarme. Voy a marcar alergia a mariscos y maní con cocina…
Nunca adivines. Si no estás segura: “Let me check with the chef and come right back.”

4. Recomendaciones & upselling suave

Say this · Recommend
If you like citrus flavors, the passion-fruit mojito pairs very well with the fish.
For dessert, our tres leches is the house favorite — would you like me to save two portions?
Si le gustan los sabores cítricos, el mojito de maracuyá…

Upsell = una opción clara + beneficio. Nunca empujés tres cosas a la vez.

5. Durante el servicio (check-backs)

Escenario
Steak llegó well-done aunque pidieron medium-rare. Guest está molesto.
Say this · Recovery
I’m sorry — that’s not what you ordered. I’ll have the kitchen remake it medium-rare right away, and this dish is on us.
Can I bring you a complimentary appetizer while you wait?
Disculpe — no es lo que pidió. Voy a pedir que lo rehagan medium-rare…

6. La cuenta, propinas y despedida

Say this · Check & tip (neutral)
Whenever you’re ready, I can bring the check. Would you like it together or separate?
We accept cards and cash. Tips are appreciated but never required — thank you for dining with us.
Have a wonderful evening, and we hope to see you again.
Cuando gusten, les traigo la cuenta. ¿Juntas o separadas?
En inglés de servicio: “check” (EE.UU.) / “bill” (UK). Ambos funcionan; sé consistente.

7. Turnos bilingües rápidos

Practicá cambiar de idioma en el mismo turno sin mezclar mal:

  • Guest habla inglés → respondé 100% inglés
  • Guest mezcla → seguí el idioma dominante de la última frase
  • Si no entendiste: “Could you repeat that a little slower, please?”
Practice pairs
“Do you have anything without dairy?” → “Yes — the grilled vegetables and the tomato salad are dairy-free.”
“¿Me puede traer más pan?” → “Of course — more bread coming right up.” / “Claro, ya le traigo más pan.”
