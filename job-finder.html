Job Finder

Infinity Studio's employability and career development initiative — connecting participants with real hiring expectations and industry insights.

"Learn what companies are looking for and how to prepare for it."

Satellite program: Job Finder is not part of Foundations or ORT. It supports employability and exposure within the Infinity ecosystem.

What it is

The program invites recruiters, hiring managers, trainers, supervisors and industry professionals to share their experiences and insights. Participants gain exposure to real hiring expectations, labor market trends and professional development strategies.

Topics include

Resume advice
LinkedIn optimization
Interview preparation
Hiring trends
Industry expectations
Career growth
Recruitment insights
Job opportunities

Primary goal

Help participants understand how to position themselves for real employment opportunities.
