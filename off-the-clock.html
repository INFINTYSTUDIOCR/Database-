Off The Clock

Infinity Studio's live talk show — a relaxed space for students, trainers and guests to connect beyond the classroom.

"Learn who we are beyond the classroom."

Satellite program: Off The Clock is not part of Foundations or ORT. It is an independent community initiative that supports engagement and brand connection within the Infinity ecosystem.

Purpose

Build community, engagement and brand connection. The objective is not teaching — it is conversation, entertainment, interaction and relationship building.

What happens

Participants get to know the people behind Infinity, share experiences, discuss current topics and engage in authentic communication in a relaxed environment.

Typical topics

Current events
Technology & AI
Work experiences
Business
Culture
Personal stories
Trending topics

Primary goal

Create community and strengthen relationships with current and future students.
