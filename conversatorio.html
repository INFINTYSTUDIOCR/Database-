The Conversatory

An invitation-only live communication event — authentic conversations that showcase the Infinity methodology in action.

"Come practice with us and experience the method firsthand."

Satellite program: The Conversatory is not part of Foundations or ORT. It is a live practice and visibility initiative within the Infinity ecosystem.

What it is

Participants from different institutions are invited to join Infinity students in moderated live discussions. Sessions are broadcast live on social media platforms such as TikTok.

This is not a lecture. Not a webinar. Not a class. It is a live communication experience in a supportive environment.

What participants can do

  • Practice English in authentic conversations
  • Interact with Infinity students
  • Experience the methodology firsthand
  • Build confidence and challenge themselves
  • Improve communication skills in real time

Primary goal

Provide authentic communication practice while publicly demonstrating the effectiveness of the Infinity approach.

Request an invitation

The Conversatory is invitation-only. Apply below and we'll contact you via WhatsApp.
